Our Story

With reach across all 50 states, we have built a reputation for connecting top talent with quality employers nationwide. Our expertise spans more than 40 industries, supporting roles from entry-level positions to executive placements. Founded by a staffing professional who began their career at Robert Half, our company was created to deliver top-tier service at more accessible and competitive fees.

What sets us apart is our commitment to providing the highest quality talent at the most reasonable rates. We believe that exceptional staffing shouldn't come at an exceptional price.

Family-owned and nationally trusted, Blue Chip Staffing is your partner for growth.

Our Core Values

Quality First

We never compromise on the quality of talent we place. Every candidate is thoroughly vetted and matched to your specific needs.

National Reach

With a nationwide presence across all 50 states, we combine local expertise with national scale to deliver tailored staffing solutions wherever you need us.

Family Values

As a family-owned business, we treat every client, candidate, and team member like family. Your success is our success.

Industry Expertise

With decades of combined experience across dozens of industries, we understand the unique staffing challenges of your sector.

Diversity & Inclusion

At Blue Chip Staffing, we believe that diversity and inclusion in the workplace cause all employees to feel accepted and valued. When employees feel accepted and valued, they are also happier in their workplace and stay longer with a company.

Our commitment to ethical staffing practices ensures that every candidate is evaluated on their skills, experience, and potential — creating diverse teams that drive innovation and deliver stronger business results.

Companies with greater diversity have lower turnover rates, higher employee morale, and better decision-making across the board. We're proud to champion these values in every placement we make.
