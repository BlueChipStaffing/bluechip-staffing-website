Parker Singha

Technical Recruiter

Parker Singha serves as a Technical Recruiter at Blue Chip Staffing LLC, where he has been part of the team since May 2024. In this role, Parker focuses on identifying and placing technical professionals across a range of industries, supporting clients with their specialized hiring needs. He manages the recruitment process from sourcing and candidate engagement through interview coordination and placement, working closely with both clients and candidates to ensure strong matches. Parker is known for his proactive approach to talent sourcing and his ability to build relationships with technical professionals while helping organizations secure the skilled talent needed to support their growth.
