Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Blue Chip Staffing different from other staffing agencies?

+

Blue Chip Staffing combines 20+ years of industry experience with a family-owned approach that prioritizes long-term relationships over quick placements. We provide personalized service, comprehensive vetting of candidates, and ongoing support to ensure the best match between employers and job seekers.

How long does it typically take to fill a position?

+

Time-to-fill depends on the role complexity and requirements. For contract positions, we typically place candidates within 3-7 business days. Direct hire placements may take 2-4 weeks due to the thorough vetting process. Our extensive talent network and rapid candidate screening ensure faster placements than industry averages.

What is the difference between contract staffing and direct hire placement?

+

Contract staffing provides temporary workforce solutions for specific projects or seasonal needs, with employees on our payroll. Direct hire placement finds permanent employees for your organization. Contract-to-hire combines both—employees start as contractors and transition to permanent roles if both parties agree. Choose based on your budget, timeline, and hiring needs.

How does Blue Chip screen and vet candidates?

+

Our rigorous vetting process includes skills assessments, background checks, reference verification, and behavioral interviews. We verify work history, education, and industry certifications. For specialized roles, technical assessments ensure candidates meet specific requirements. We only present candidates who meet our high standards and are genuinely suited for the position.

Do you provide ongoing support after placement?

+

Yes, we're committed to placement success. We provide a 90-day placement guarantee for direct hires, monitor performance in contract roles, and maintain regular communication with both clients and placed employees. If issues arise, we work quickly to resolve them or provide replacement candidates at no additional cost.

What industries and specialties does Blue Chip serve?

+

We specialize in Corporate & Professional, Construction, Hospitality, Artificial Intelligence, Venture Capital/Private Equity, Life Sciences, and Quality Control & QA roles. Our expertise spans multiple sectors nationwide, with deep industry knowledge and established networks in each specialty. Contact us to discuss your specific staffing needs.
